buying:
  - heading: What we look for
    body:
      - >-
        Attribution to the standard references, with the reference cited in
        the entry so that a buyer can check it. Where a coin carries
        third-party grading we give the grading service and the certificate
        number rather than simply repeating the grade.
      - >-
        Surface work is the field's recurring problem. Tooling, smoothing,
        re-engraving and modern patination are common on ancient coins and are
        frequently undisclosed. We examine surfaces under magnification and
        report what we find.
  - heading: Findspots and the law
    body:
      - >-
        For coins found in England and Wales, the Treasure Act 1996 governs
        what must be reported, and material that has been through the process
        carries a record. Where a findspot or a recording reference exists we
        give it.
      - >-
        Hoard coins without any recorded findspot are a different proposition
        and are described as such.
  - heading: Worth asking
    body:
      - >-
        What is the attribution reference. Has the surface been tooled or
        smoothed. If it is slabbed, by whom and under what number. Is there a
        recorded findspot.
