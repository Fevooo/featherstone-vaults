buying:
  - heading: What we look for
    body:
      - >-
        Material that can be tied to a named individual or unit through
        surviving records. A medal is a piece of metal; a medal with the
        service record behind it is a biography, and the research is most of
        the work.
      - >-
        Naming is the first thing checked. Erased and re-impressed naming on
        medals is a known practice and can be convincing. We examine naming
        under magnification against the correct style for the issue and we
        report what we find.
  - heading: Groups and records
    body:
      - >-
        Groups are kept together wherever possible. Breaking a group for
        margin destroys the thing that made it worth having, and it cannot be
        undone.
      - >-
        Copies of the records consulted are supplied with the object: service
        papers, medal rolls, unit diaries and any photographs or documents
        that came with it. Where research has not been completed we say what
        has been checked and what has not.
  - heading: Worth asking
    body:
      - >-
        Is the naming original to the medal. Is the group as issued, or has it
        been assembled. Which records were consulted, and are copies supplied.
        Are any elements replacements.
